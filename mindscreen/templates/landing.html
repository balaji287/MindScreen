Understand Student Well-being Through Data.

MindScreen looks at five kinds of everyday data (screen time, behaviour, physical wellness, social interaction and academic life) instead of relying on a single questionnaire, and shows patterns that may deserve attention. It is a screening and decision-support aid, never a diagnosis.

How it works

1

Collect Data

Upload a CSV per category (templates provided) or add a day manually. Every file is validated and previewed before it is saved.

2

Analyse Patterns

One unified pipeline cleans and combines the available data, tracks trends against your own baseline and, when enough validated data exists, runs the prediction model.

3

Review Insights

See trends with honest gaps, a screening category with plain-language explanation, and a report you can share if you choose.

Privacy and responsible use

Consent comes first and is separate for optional wearable and grade data. MindScreen only uses aggregate numbers: never private messages, contact lists, passwords, browsing content or precise location. Students see only their own data; researchers see only aggregates, and small groups are hidden. You can delete your data or account at any time, and you can use the platform without being labelled or penalised.

MindScreen is a research prototype. Its model is trained on a public survey dataset and is not clinically validated. If you are struggling, please talk to a counsellor or doctor.
