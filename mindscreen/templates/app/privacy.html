How your data is protected

  • Analysis uses a pseudonymous ID; your email is not stored with your records.
  • Role-based access: students see only their own data. Researchers see aggregates only, and groups smaller than 5 are hidden.
  • Uploads are limited to 2 MB CSV files, validated on the server, and the original is stored separately from cleaned records.
  • Upload, validation, deletion and report events are logged (pseudonymous, no content).
  • You can use MindScreen without being labelled or penalised. Results are not shared with anyone unless you choose to share a report.
